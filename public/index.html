find_nearby_experiences
Location-based discovery — find bookable experiences near any lat/lng coordinates within a configurable radius. Perfect for "things to do near me" queries.

Browse all 680+ cities with available experiences. Helps AI assistants guide users through destination exploration and trip planning.

Deep dive into a specific experience — available dates, variant pricing, venue location with coordinates, and feature images. Accepts a tickadoo slug for seamless chaining from search results.

How It Works

Connect your AI application to tickadoo's live inventory of theatre, shows, events, tours and experiences across 680+ cities. The server speaks the open Model Context Protocol standard — search by city or coordinates, get real-time pricing and availability, and link users directly to booking pages on tickadoo.com. Pair with the product feed (1.8M+ rows, OpenAI Commerce spec) for deeper catalogue integration. No API keys required. Stateless, fast, and ready to go. For crawler-friendly discovery, see llms.txt.
